AgentDebugX is a framework-agnostic debugging toolkit for LLM agents, multi-agent systems, tool-using agents, and computer-use agents.

Its Diagnose → Rerun workflow detects visible failures, attributes root causes to responsible steps or agents, proposes evidence-backed recovery actions, and validates fixes through controlled reruns.
