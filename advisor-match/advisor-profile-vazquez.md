# Faculty Profile: Prof. Marynel Vázquez — Yale CS

**Position:** Assistant Professor
**Institution:** Yale University, Department of Computer Science
**Lab:** Interactive Machines Group
**Report date:** 2026-06-12

---

## Research Focus
Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) in multi-party settings, graph-based social reasoning for robots, spatial behavior patterns, LLM applied to HRI contexts. Key idea: abstracting human-robot interactions as graphs to reason about individual, relationship, and group factors.

## Academic Profile
- PhD: Carnegie Mellon University (Robotics, 2017)
- NSF CAREER Award (2022)
- Two Amazon Research Awards
